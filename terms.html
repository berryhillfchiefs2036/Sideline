Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

What we collect

  • Coach accounts: email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider). Optionally a display name shown on the play log.
  • Team data entered by coaches: player first names and jersey numbers, positions, play-by-play events, game scores, and schedules. We ask coaches not to enter last names, birthdates, or any other personal details about players.
  • Technical basics needed to run the service (such as authentication tokens and request logs kept by our hosting providers).

What we do with it

We use this information only to provide the service: syncing games between the coaches on a crew, showing the read-only gamecast to people who hold the watch link, and computing season stats. We do not sell personal information, we do not run advertising, and we do not share team data with anyone outside the crew except as required by law.

Children

Sideline accounts are for adults. Information about players (first name and jersey number) is entered by their coaches, and the team's organization is responsible for having whatever permission it needs from parents or guardians to record it — the same permission that covers a paper stat book or a league website. Parents or guardians can ask their team's coach, or contact us at [your contact email], to have a player's information corrected or removed.

Where it lives

Data is stored with our infrastructure providers, Supabase (database and authentication) and Vercel (hosting), which maintain their own security and privacy programs. A copy of your crew's data is also cached on each coach's own phone so the app works without signal.

Retention and deletion

Team data stays until the team's coaches delete it. To delete an account or a whole crew's data, contact [your contact email] and we will remove it within 30 days.
