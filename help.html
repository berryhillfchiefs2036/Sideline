How to use Sideline

From first install to a full season

1 · Set up your team

  • Install it like an app. Open the site on your phone, then use your browser's Add to Home Screen. It works offline once installed.
  • Add players on the Roster tab — number and name are all you need. "Paste a whole roster" takes a list like 12 Jordan Blair, one per line.
  • Set starters on the Lineups tab for offense, defense, and each special teams unit. Tap a position's name to rename it to whatever your playbook calls it.
  • Name your team and add the schedule on the Season tab. Check Scrimmage on practice games — they stay out of the record and season stats.
  • Pick your scoring level on the Roster tab: elementary (kick +2, run/pass +1) or high school (flipped).

2 · Coach together

  • Tap the bar at the very top to open the crew screen, and create your free coach account (email and password — one account per coach, for every team you coach).
  • The first coach taps Create a code — that makes the crew and brings the roster and schedule along. That coach is the crew's owner.
  • Every other coach signs into their own account and types in the crew code once. From then on, all phones share one game: one score, one play log, one roster.
  • Split the work however you like — one coach subs, one keeps down & distance, one logs plays. Each phone browses freely; nothing moves another coach's screen.
  • The owner can see every coach on the crew screen and remove anyone who shouldn't be there.
  • It's your data, start to finish: removing a player can also erase their name from every saved game, and the owner can delete the whole team — everything, permanently — right from the crew screen. No emails, no waiting.

3 · Game day

  • Start the game: the Game tab asks "Which game is this?" — tap your scheduled game (or track without one). Plays land in the right game automatically.
  • Log a play: tap the player's card, pick what happened, the yards, and any points. Passes auto-credit the QB (changeable for trick plays); tackles take assists.
  • Snap, no stat counts a play for everyone on the field without a stat line — keeps play minimums honest.
  • Flag logs penalties with the type, the player, and the yards — down & distance walk off automatically (and stay manually editable, always).
  • TD+ on their side of the scoreboard logs the other team's scores, punts, and failed conversion tries.
  • Quarters: tap the Q chip to end a quarter or the half. Ball spot is optional — turn it on and it tracks the ball down the field play by play.
  • Sub and Move on any player card swap kids in and out; the chain under each card fills toward the team's play minimum.

4 · Fix anything

  • Every line in the play log has buttons to move (reorder), insert a missed play after it, edit it (player, yards, points, quarter, even who was on the field), or remove it. Score, downs, and stats recompute instantly.
  • Forgot to end a quarter? Edit the first play of the new quarter and mark "Quarter N starts at this play."
  • Ended a game too soon, or found a mistake later? Reopen any saved game from the Season tab (the board must be clear) — every play comes back, fully editable. End it again when you're done.

5 · Ending games, stats, and the season

  • End game shows the opponent, the date, and which scheduled game gets marked final — confirm and the game archives to the Season tab, and the board clears for the next one.
  • The Stats tab tracks the live game: play counts, offense/defense/special tables, drives, first downs, third downs, and turnover margin — plus Share box score for the team group chat and a CSV download.
  • The Season tab keeps the record, season totals for every player (including the game on the board), and every saved game — tap Stats on any of them for its full read-out. Back up now and then; Restore merges backups.

6 · Let fans watch

On the crew screen, tap Copy the watch link and share it anywhere. Fans get a live, view-only gamecast — the field with the ball spot, down & distance, the current drive, play-by-play, and a full game-stats tab. No account needed, and nothing a fan does can change the game.
