Sideline

The game-day tracker built on an actual sideline

Sideline is a play-by-play and stats tracker for youth football, built by coaches who needed one — designed around what a real sideline is like: loud, fast, sunny, and staffed by volunteers holding phones.

A whole coaching staff works one game together: one coach subs players, another keeps down & distance, another logs plays — every phone stays in sync. Every kid's snap counts toward the play minimum, every carry, catch, tackle, kick, flag, and score is captured, and the season adds itself up.

What makes it different

  • Built for many hands. Multiple coaches log the same game at once from their own phones.
  • Everything is fixable. Any play can be edited, inserted, reordered, or removed — even after a game has ended. Finished games reopen for full editing.
  • Fans follow live. A view-only gamecast — field graphic, drive tracker, play-by-play, and live stats — with a link that can't change anything.
  • Play minimums front and center. Every player card shows their snap count against the team minimum, so no kid gets forgotten.
  • Careers, not just games. Player stats carry across games and seasons, survive jersey changes, and export to CSV whenever you want them.
